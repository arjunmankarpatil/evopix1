FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Evopix free?

Yes. You can download and use Evopix for free.

Which phones are supported?

Android 10 and newer, on phones and tablets.

Is it safe? Why does Android show a warning?

Android shows a standard warning for any app installed from outside the Play Store. Evopix asks only for access to your photos, so you can pick pictures to edit. Nothing is uploaded.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every tool works offline.

Where do my projects go?

They are stored inside the app on your phone. Save a .zip copy from the editor menu to back them up or move them to another phone.

Is it coming to Google Play?

Yes, it is being tested on Google Play right now. This page always has the latest download.
