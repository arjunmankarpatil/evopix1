1About this policy

Evopix (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a photo editing app for Android published by Evopix. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. We wrote it to be short and clear, because the honest answer is simple: Evopix does not collect, transmit, share or sell your personal information.

2What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. Here is what that means in practice:

Type of dataCollected by us?
Name, email address or account detailsNo — the app has no accounts
Your photos and edited imagesNo — they stay on your device
Location, contacts, microphone or camera dataNo — the app never asks for them
Device identifiers, advertising IDNo
Usage analytics or crash reports sent to usNo — the app has no analytics tools
Payment informationNo — the app has no purchases
The app does not request the internet permission, so it has no way of sending photos or any other data off your device.

3Permissions the app uses

  • Photos and media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and older): used only so you can browse your gallery and choose pictures to edit. A picture is read on your device when you select it, and it is not uploaded anywhere.

Evopix asks for no other permissions. In particular it does not use your contacts, location, microphone, camera, calendar or phone.

Some photos contain hidden details such as the date or place they were taken. Evopix does not read those details to send them anywhere, because it never sends anything.

4Files saved on your device

To let you continue your work later, the app saves things on your device only:

  • Your editing projects, including layers, and the appincluding layers and history, and the app’s auto-saved copiesrsquo;s auto-saved copies
  • Your settings, such as theme, layout choice and tool options
  • Pictures you choose to export or save

These files stay on your phone, under your control. We cannot see or access them.

5Built-in library

Shapes, arrows, flares, fire, explosions, skies and other creative assets are bundled inside the app. Using them needs no network connection and sends no information anywhere.

6Sharing and exporting

We do not share your data with anyone, because we never receive it. If you choose to share or send a picture using Android’s share options, the picture goes to the app you pick, such as a messaging or social app, and is handled under that app’s own privacy policy.

7Your control

  • Delete your work: delete projects inside the app, or delete exported pictures from your gallery.
  • Remove everything: uninstalling Evopix removes the app’s saved projects and settings from your device.
  • Change permissions: you can switch off photo access any time in Android Settings → Apps → Evopix → Permissions. The app will then ask again when you next want to pick a photo.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on our side. If you still have a question about your rights, contact us below.

8Children

Evopix is a general-purpose photo editor and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9Security

Since your data never leaves your device, its safety depends on your device’s own protections: a screen lock, up-to-date Android, and care about which apps you install. We keep the app simple on purpose, with no accounts or servers, so there is no database of user data that could be exposed.

10Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding a feature that uses the internet, we will update this policy first and change the effective date at the top of this page. Please check back from time to time.
