01Summary in plain words

Evopix is a photo editor that works entirely on your phone. Here is everything important in a few lines:

  • We do not collect your personal information. Evopix has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking.
  • Your photos and projects stay on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • There are no ads, no analytics tools, no third-party trackers and no in-app purchases.
  • You are in full control: delete projects in the app, change permissions in Android settings, or uninstall to remove everything.
In shortThe rest of this page explains each point in detail, including the app, our website, and your rights.

04Permissions the app uses and why

Android apps must ask permission before they can reach certain things on your phone. Evopix asks for very little.

Photos and media
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
Used only so you can browse your gallery and pick a picture to edit. The picture is read on your device when you choose it. It is not uploaded anywhere. On Android 12 and older the older permission READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE does the same job.
Internet
Not requested. Evopix does not declare the internet permission, so it has no way of connecting to the network.
Camera
Not requested. The Camera button simply opens your phone’s own camera app. That app takes the photo and hands it back to Evopix, which saves it in your gallery. Evopix never accesses the camera itself.
Everything else
Contacts, location, microphone, calendar and phone permissions are not used.

You can switch photo access off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Evopix → Permissions. The app will ask again the next time you want to pick a photo.

05Files stored on your device

To let you continue your work later, Evopix saves a few things on your phone. They are stored inside the app’s private area and in your gallery, under your control:

Projects
Your editing projects, including layers and history, and the app’s automatic saved copies.
Settings
Choices such as theme, layout, UI size, brush defaults and auto-rotate.
Imported fonts and looks
Font files and colour-look (.cube) files you choose to import.
Exported pictures
Pictures you export or save. They appear in your gallery like any other photo.
Photos taken with Camera
Saved to your gallery by the camera flow described above.

We cannot see or reach any of these files. Uninstalling the app removes the projects and settings that are stored inside it. Pictures you exported to your gallery stay there until you delete them.

06Built-in library and templates

Templates, shapes, arrows, flares, fire, explosions, skies and other creative assets are bundled inside the app. Using them needs no network connection and sends no information anywhere.

07Sharing, disclosure and third parties

We do not share your data with anyone because we never receive it. We do not sell data, we do not use advertising networks and we do not include third-party tracking tools in the app.

If you choose to share or send a picture using Android’s share options, the picture goes to the app you pick, such as a messaging or social app. From that moment it is handled under that app’s own privacy policy.

We may disclose information only if the law requires it. Since the app holds no personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to hand over.

08Google Play and your download

Evopix is available through Google Play (currently in testing) and as a direct download from evopix.in. If you get the app from Google Play, Google may process information about your download, device and account under Google’s own privacy policy. That information is handled by Google, and we do not control it. The app itself does not send anything to Google or to us.

If you download the file from evopix.in, it is delivered by GitHub. See the next section.

09Our website evopix.in

The website is a simple static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, advertising or analytics tools, and it does not ask you to create an account or fill in forms. Its fonts and images are part of the page, so it does not load them from other companies.

Like any website host, GitHub may keep technical logs such as your IP address and browser type when you visit the site or download the app, for security and operation. This is under GitHub’s privacy statement, and we do not receive those logs. When you email us, we receive your email address and message so we can reply.

10Your choices and rights

Delete your work
Delete projects inside the app, or delete exported pictures from your gallery.
Remove everything
Uninstall Evopix to remove its saved projects and settings from your device.
Change permissions
Turn photo access off or on in Android Settings → Apps → Evopix → Permissions.
Access, correct, erase
We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that email thread and we will.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion and the right to complain to a regulator. Because Evopix does not collect personal data, these rights are met by design. You can still contact us at any time with a question or request.

11Children’s privacy

Evopix is a general-purpose photo editor and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects nothing, no child’s information is collected through it. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please write to us.

12Security and retention

Your data never leaves your device, so its safety depends on your phone’s own protections: a screen lock, an up-to-date Android and care about which apps you install. We keep the app simple on purpose, with no accounts and no servers, so there is no database of user data that could be exposed or leaked.

Retention: we keep nothing about you. Projects and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.
